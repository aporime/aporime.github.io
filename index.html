01 / WEB ARCHITECTURE

Design & print at retail scale

The workflow imported designer-created templates, let customers customize them with real-time rendered previews, and generated final print-ready files for delivery to print facilities. The work covered the overall architecture and 2D rendering optimization across that pipeline.
